Easygoing · Class I–II

River Explorer

A gentle introduction to the river, with calm stretches, small splashes, and time to take in the canyon. Your guide introduces paddle techniques before leading the crew downstream.

Ideal for families and first-time rafters. Includes a riverside snack break.

Adventure · Class II–III

Canyon Rush

Make a half day of it with rolling waves, narrow canyon passages, and quieter water between the rapids. Practice working as a team as your guide calls each paddle stroke.

For active beginners and returning rafters. Includes a picnic lunch beside the river.

Whitewater Challenge

Spend a full day tackling bigger waves and technical stretches. Start with a skills briefing, then put your teamwork to use through a series of energetic rapids and scenic river sections.

Every trip includes a guide, raft, paddle, helmet, fitted life jacket, a safety briefing, and transport back to the meeting point.

Rapid River Rafting

Our mission is to provide safe and exciting river adventures that connect people with nature and create lasting memories.

History

Rapid River Rafting began in 2015 when two friends started guiding small groups down their favorite local river. Their love for adventure and safety helped the company grow into a welcoming place for families, friends, and new explorers.
